 For thousands of generations, human evolution crawled forward beneath a pristine sky, birthed by the very cradle of astronomy. But modern humanity has choked the earth with artificial light and noise, blinding itself to its origins—prompting the universe to slowly withdraw its ancient beauty from our sight forever. Yet tonight, in a rare act of cosmic error, the power grid failed, allowing a 16-year-old youth, isolated in a dark village, to witness what humanity has lost: a sky burning with thousands of ancient stars, pierced only by the silent, rapid racing of man-made satellites.
 This is a night that does not belong to everyone; it is a sacred, terrifying gift reserved only for the chosen, fortunate youth.
